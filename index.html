Clear about consequences

Guardrails where they matter.

Powerful transfer modes can delete or move data. RobustCopy puts their effects in front of you before execution.

Safer destructive operations

Mirror, Purge, and Move modes remain available, but they never hide what they are allowed to change.

  • List-only pre-scan estimates destination deletions before the transfer.
  • A mandatory confirmation explains planned deletions and source moves.
  • Source and destination cannot be identical or contain one another.
  • Robocopy starts directly without routing arguments through a command shell.
Always maintain an independent backup. Review the command and destination carefully before using `/MIR`, `/PURGE`, `/MOV`, or `/MOVE`.

Privacy, kept local

RobustCopy does not send your files, paths, configuration, or transcripts to Kaleb Creative Studio. Logs stay on your device until you delete them.
