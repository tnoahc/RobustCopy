# RobustCopy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 7, 2026

**Last updated:** August 7, 2026

**Publisher:** Kaleb Creative Studio

## 1. At a glance

RobustCopy is a Windows desktop application for configuring and running the Windows `robocopy.exe` file-transfer utility.

RobustCopy runs locally on your device. The publisher does not collect or receive your files, folder paths, transfer history, usage data, or application logs through the app. RobustCopy has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or publisher-operated cloud service.

| Data practice | What RobustCopy does |
| --- | --- |
| Publisher collection through the app | None |
| Local file access | Only locations and transfer options you select |
| Local storage | Transfer transcripts stored on your device |
| Automatic diagnostics or telemetry | None |
| Publisher-operated network transmission | None |
| Advertising, tracking, or in-app cookies | None |
| Sale or advertising-related sharing | None |
| User control | Choose locations and options; delete transcripts at any time |

## 2. Scope

This policy explains how the RobustCopy desktop application accesses and handles information. It does not cover the privacy practices of Windows, a software distribution platform, your email provider, your network administrator, or any storage provider or network location that you choose to use with RobustCopy.

In this policy, information that RobustCopy **processes locally** is information the app reads or uses on your device to perform a requested transfer. Local processing does not mean that the publisher collects, receives, or can access that information.

If an employer, school, or other organization provides or manages your device, account, network, source, or destination, that organization may be able to access files and transcripts under its own policies. Contact the organization for information about its practices.

## 3. Information handled by RobustCopy

RobustCopy may process the following information locally on your device:

- **Selected files and folders:** The source and destination folders you choose, together with the files, subfolders, names, paths, sizes, timestamps, attributes, and other file-system metadata needed to perform the transfer.
- **Transfer configuration:** File patterns, selected Robocopy options, advanced command switches, and the generated command preview.
- **Transfer activity:** Pre-scan results, progress information, filenames, full paths, byte counts, status messages, errors, timestamps, and Robocopy exit results.
- **Local transcripts:** Text logs created for scans and transfers.

This information may contain personal, confidential, or sensitive information depending on the files, folder names, and locations you select. RobustCopy processes it only to perform the actions you request.

RobustCopy does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, advertising identifier, or online accounts.

## 4. How information is used

RobustCopy uses locally processed information to:

- validate the selected source, destination, and transfer options;
- preview the Robocopy command;
- estimate files, bytes, and planned deletions before a transfer;
- copy or move files between locations you select;
- display progress, results, warnings, and errors; and
- create a local transcript for troubleshooting and your records.

RobustCopy does not use this information for profiling, advertising, marketing, or automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 5. Local storage and retention

RobustCopy saves transfer transcripts as unencrypted UTF-8 text files in:

`%LocalAppData%\RobustCopy\Logs`

Transcripts may include full paths, filenames, commands, timestamps, transfer output, and error details. They remain on your device until you delete them. RobustCopy does not automatically upload or automatically delete them.

When upgrading from an earlier version, RobustCopy may copy existing transcripts from `%LocalAppData%\RoboCopyGUI\Logs` into the current log directory. The original legacy transcripts are not deleted automatically.

Uninstalling RobustCopy may not remove these log folders. You can review or delete either folder using Windows File Explorer.

## 6. Network locations and data transmission

RobustCopy does not transmit information to the publisher or to a publisher-operated server.

If you select a mapped drive, UNC path, network share, remote device, or cloud-synchronized folder as a source or destination, Windows and Robocopy will transfer or access data using that location and your existing credentials. That transfer is initiated by your selection and occurs between the locations you control or choose. The destination's operator, your network administrator, or your storage provider may process the transferred information under their own policies.

## 7. Clipboard and Windows integration

RobustCopy uses Windows components to provide the folder picker, run Robocopy, open files or folders, and copy text to the clipboard. A transcript is placed on the Windows clipboard only when you choose the **Copy** action. Clipboard contents may be available to other software or services on your device according to your Windows configuration.

RobustCopy contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software development kits.

If you download RobustCopy through a software store or another distribution platform, that platform may independently process download, licensing, payment, diagnostic, or account information under its own privacy policy. The publisher does not control that platform's processing through the RobustCopy application.

## 8. Disclosure, sale, and targeted advertising

The app does not independently disclose your locally processed files, paths, configuration, or transcripts to the publisher or third parties. The only file transfer performed by RobustCopy is the transfer you direct between your selected source and destination. The publisher does not sell personal information handled by RobustCopy, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

If you send information directly to the publisher for support, the publisher may disclose it to service providers that operate the relevant communication or support systems, when required by applicable law or valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the app, or legal rights. The publisher does not permit service providers to use support information for their own advertising purposes.

## 9. Security

RobustCopy relies on your Windows account, file-system permissions, device security, and network configuration to protect files and local transcripts. Transcript files are not encrypted by RobustCopy. Anyone or any software with sufficient access to your Windows account or file system may be able to read them.

You are responsible for selecting appropriate source and destination locations, protecting your Windows account, securing network shares, and deleting transcripts that you no longer need.

## 10. Your choices and controls

You control the information processed by RobustCopy by choosing:

- whether to run the app;
- which source and destination locations to use;
- which files and transfer options to include;
- whether to copy a transcript to the clipboard; and
- when to delete locally stored transcripts.

Because the publisher does not receive the app's locally processed information, the publisher cannot view, retrieve, correct, export, or delete that information for you. You can manage it directly through RobustCopy and Windows File Explorer.

## 11. Information you send to the publisher

RobustCopy has no in-app account or support form. If you voluntarily contact the publisher by email or another support channel, the publisher may receive your contact details, message, and any diagnostic information or transcripts you choose to provide.

The publisher will use that information to respond to your request, investigate problems, maintain the app, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law. Please remove confidential filenames, paths, or file contents before sharing a transcript. Support communications will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and may be processed by the communication provider you choose and the publisher's service provider.

For personal information sent directly to the publisher, Kaleb Creative Studio is the controller or responsible organization. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the publisher relies, as appropriate, on steps taken at your request or performance of a contract, its legitimate interests in supporting and securing RobustCopy, and compliance with legal obligations.

Where applicable law grants you rights concerning personal information held by the publisher, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection by using the contact details below. These rights may be subject to legal limitations. You may also complain to the data-protection authority responsible for your location.

## 12. AI-assisted development and documentation

Generative artificial intelligence tools assisted with parts of RobustCopy's development, testing, branding, documentation, Store listing materials, and this privacy policy. Human review and decisions remain the responsibility of Kaleb Creative Studio.

RobustCopy does not contain an artificial intelligence model, connect to an AI service, or use AI to analyze transfers. The application does not send users' files, folder paths, transfer settings, transcripts, or other locally processed information to an AI provider.

AI assistance used during development is separate from operation of the released application. Information that a user independently chooses to provide through a support channel is handled as described in Section 11; users should not send confidential files, paths, or transcripts unless specifically necessary and should remove sensitive information first.

## 13. Children

RobustCopy is a general-purpose file-transfer utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect or transmit children's personal information to the publisher.

## 14. International transfers

RobustCopy does not transfer app data to the publisher across national borders. Data may cross borders if you choose a remote or cloud-synchronized source or destination, or if you contact the publisher using a provider that processes communications in another country.
